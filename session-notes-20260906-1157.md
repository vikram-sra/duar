## 1. Traps that cost real time this session

Read this section before touching lighting, wind, foliage, or the floor.

### `this.time` advances PER FRAME, not per second
- `main.js`: `this.time += 0.001` once per `animate()`. At 60 fps that is 0.06/s.
- `uWindTime = this.time * 24` advances **1.44 units/second, not 24**.
- A wind coefficient of `1.5` is ~0.34 Hz, not 5.7 Hz. Misreading this as seconds put every rate out by 16.7x, causing canopies to appear frozen when trying to calm them down.
- **Note:** Wind and day cycles remain frame-rate dependent (runs ~2x faster on 120 Hz displays).

### Wind amplitude must scale to deformed geometry, never model height
- `transformed` in the vertex shader is **pre-instance**.
- Neem's leaf clusters are GPU-instanced: geometry is a ~3-unit clump inside a 2,074-unit tall model, scaled by `instanceMatrix`.
- Sizing amplitude from model height displaced every clump by ~185% of itself, and the instance matrix multiplied that again, shredding the canopy into screen streaks.
- Scaling displacement to `geometry.boundingBox` cancels instance scale.

### Rigid motion is nearly invisible; relative motion is what reads
- A spatial wind wave with an 84m wavelength across a 20m canopy moves the tree as an almost rigid block.
- Foliage reads as moving due to *relative differential motion* between branches and clusters.
- The branch term (`WIND_BRANCH_*` / `brWave1`, `brWave2`) introduces phase variance across limbs to create organic billowing.

### Wide directional shadow frustums erase thin botanical features
- The scene's main `sunLight` has an 85m half-frustum (170m x 170m coverage) for tree canopies. At 3072 map size, that is ~5.5 cm per texel.
- With `normalBias: 0.018`, delicate features like 1cm rose stems and thin petals suffer from peter-panning and texel under-sampling, casting virtually invisible shadows.
- Solution: A dedicated local shadow caster (`RoseCenterpieceShadowLight`) with a tight 3.4m frustum (1024x1024, ~3.3 mm/texel) tracking celestial coordinates provides millimeter-crisp petal and stem shadows.

### DoubleSide materials require explicit `applyFoliageSunShading`
- Stock Three.js flips normals on backfaces of `DoubleSide` materials, creating uniform exposure.
- Trees were hooked to `applyFoliageSunShading` which modulates backfaces with `uBacksideShade` tinted by skylight (`vec3(0.84, 0.93, 1.16)`).
- The rose originally bypassed this hook, rendering it flat and overly bright. Adding `applyFoliageSunShading` gave the petals and leaves rich volumetric self-shadowing.

### Transparent sorting & Z-fighting on ground contact shadows
- Setting ground contact planes at tiny offsets like `y = 0.008` with `transparent: true` causes depth sorting anomalies and Z-fighting against a 150m terrain mesh.
- Elevated to `y = 0.015`, set `renderOrder = 3`, `depthWrite: false`, and `toneMapped: false` for stable, non-flickering ground occlusion.

### A photogrammetry texture is a UV atlas, not a picture of the ground
- Tiling `forest_floor_scan.glb`'s texture drew fragmented charts separated by stretched borders.
- `scripts/bake-floor-texture.py` renders an orthographic top-down rasterisation across 100k triangles with depth buffering, extracting the largest fully-covered square cross-faded for seamless wrapping.
- `FOREST_FLOOR_UV_SCALE` must stay matched with `bake-floor-texture.py`'s reported tile span (23.09m).

### Laying a scan down as geometry always shows its crop edge
- Laying the floor scan down as a 3D mesh leaves a noticeable crop boundary in the clearing.
- Applying it as a procedural texture across the continuous ground disc eliminates boundaries and saved 100,000 triangles.

### `isFoliageMat` is not safe for vertex displacement
- Matches names like `Material.001`, which on neem is the trunk.
- Vertex wind uses strict regex gating rejecting trunk/bark/wood and requiring leaf/foliage names.

## 2. What changed in the session

### Day/Night Continuity & Contrast
- Celestial system returns continuous **`sunW`** (smoothstep of solar altitude over `-0.12 .. 0.08`). Key lights, painting spotlight, and foliage shading crossfade smoothly with no discrete threshold pops.
- Foliage shading direction transitions gracefully: `uShadeAmount` is `|sunStrength - moonStrength|`, falling to zero at equality so the flip happens when directional shading is zero.
- Cut `scene.environmentIntensity` from 0.35 to 0.13; metallic materials compensated with `envMapIntensity: 2.7`.
- Shadows darkened to **6.7% of lit brightness** (from 16.2%).
- `uBacksideShade` set to 0.15 with widened soft terminator.

### Neem Tree Motion & Real-Time Performance
- Rebuilt neem model via `scripts/build-neem-glb.py` (3.3 MB, 1,000 instanced leaf clusters, botanical color grading, GPU instanced).
- Calmed motion: reduced `speedMult` from `1.10` to `0.38`, `waveAmp` from `0.72` to `0.24`, `branchAmp` to `0.08`, `branchFreq` to `0.18`, and flutter amplitude/speed to `0.035` / `0.45`.
- Reduced trunk/limb sway in `TREE_PRESETS.neem` (`swayAmplitude: 0.03`, `swayFreqMult: 0.40`).

### Banyan and Peepal Sway
- Banyan (`speedMult: 0.32`, `waveAmp: 0.36`) and Peepal (`speedMult: 0.42`, `waveAmp: 0.40`) calibrated to slow, heavy cathedral crown sways matching their massive trunk weight.

### Centerpiece Rose Rebuild & Realism
- Replaced previous centerpiece with 72 Floribunda Roses (`public/models/red_rose_1k.glb`, 452 KB, 214k triangles, authored via `scripts/build-rose-glb.py`).
- **Petals**: Pure rich ruby/crimson red (Hue `357°`, Saturation `0.85–0.98`, Value `0.28–0.92`, mean RGB `0.86, 0.10, 0.14`).
- **Leaves**: Brightness reduced by ~55%, hue shifted from lime-yellow to deep botanical forest green (`115°–125°`, max V `0.36`).
- **Stems**: Natural dark woody green `[0.09, 0.15, 0.06]`.
- **Grounding**: Anchored 7cm in ground, stem locked against wobble, micro-sway only `0.002`.
- **Fallen Petals**: Scattered ruby petals (`0xa81422`, roughness `0.68`) with contact shadows.

### Rose Plant Shadows & Ambient Occlusion
- **Foliage Sun-Shading**: Hooked `applyFoliageSunShading` to rose materials for volumetric self-shadowing on shaded faces.
- **Ground Contact Occlusion**: Multi-stage 3.4m ground shadow disc combining soft canopy shade with deep inner root-flare ambient occlusion (`0.88` opacity, `renderOrder = 3`).
- **Dedicated Shadow Caster**: `RoseCenterpieceShadowLight` (DirectionalLight, 1024x1024, 3.4m frustum, normalBias `0.003`) dynamically tracking the sun and moon arcs in `main.js`, casting razor-sharp leaf/petal silhouettes onto the forest floor.

### Forest Floor Naturalization & Detiling
- Replaced visible tile repetition with a 3-way non-periodic FBM continuous noise blend (3 irrational rotation/scale sample passes).
- Retained uneven organic 3D terrain elevation with central clearing smoothing factor.

### Asset 404 Fixes
- Fixed file casing discrepancies in `public/portfolio/` and `public/portfolio/thumbs/` (`Not Home Yet`, `Shiv Kumar Batalvi`, `Where Time Loses Its Name` for both `.jpg` and `.webp`).

### Seamless Camera Transitions
- Smooth arc flight paths between forest mode, paintings, and central sculpture.
