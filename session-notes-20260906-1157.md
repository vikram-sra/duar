## 1. Traps that cost real time this session

Read this section before touching lighting, wind or the floor. Each of these
looks obvious in hindsight and none of them is visible from the code alone.

### `this.time` advances PER FRAME, not per second

`main.js`: `this.time += 0.001` once per `animate()`. At 60 fps that is 0.06/s.
`uWindTime = this.time * 24` therefore advances **1.44 units/second, not 24**.

A wind coefficient of `1.5` is ~0.34 Hz, not 5.7 Hz. Misreading this as seconds
put every rate out by 16.7x, and a "fix" that slowed canopies by 0.08 actually
gave them a **36-second period**, which read as the trees being completely
frozen. Two rounds were lost to it. The note now lives at the top of
`injectFoliageWind`.

Consequence worth knowing: **the wind and the day cycle are frame-rate
dependent.** On a 120 Hz display everything runs about twice as fast. This
predates all of this session's work and is NOT fixed.

### Wind amplitude must scale to the deformed geometry, never to model height

`transformed` in the vertex shader is **pre-instance**. Neem's leaf clusters are
GPU-instanced: the geometry is one ~3-unit clump inside a model 2,074 units
tall, and `instanceMatrix` then scatters and scales it. Sizing amplitude from
model height displaced every clump by ~185% of itself, the instance scale
multiplied that again, and the canopy shredded into streaks across the screen.

Scaling to `geometry.boundingBox` instead makes the instance scale cancel out --
clump and displacement are multiplied by the same factor. `applyGlbFoliageWind`
does this. The other three species are authored near metre scale, so they came
out at 0.2-0.3% and looked fine while the scene was visibly broken; a
world-space measurement said "4.4 cm on every tree" and passed. **Measure
displacement as a fraction of the geometry being deformed, not in world units.**

### Rigid motion is nearly invisible; relative motion is what reads

The wave has an 84 m wavelength, so across one 20 m canopy it is almost
constant and the whole tree slides as a rigid body. 0.34-0.48 m of that was
still perceived as "not moving". Foliage reads as moving because of relative
motion *between* its parts, which is why neem looked right at a fraction of the
amplitude. Hence the branch term (`WIND_BRANCH_*`).

Its tuning is a direct trade: more cycles across the mesh means more
branch-to-branch variation **and** more phase difference across each individual
leaf, which stretches it. 2.5 cycles holds within-leaf stretch near 15%.

### A photogrammetry texture is a UV atlas, not a picture of the ground

Tiling `forest_floor_scan.glb`'s own texture across the floor drew the **atlas
layout** -- coherent patches separated by bands of smeared chart, because charts
are packed arbitrarily and stretched wherever the scan was steep. No repeat
scale fixes this; the image was never a view of the floor from above.

`scripts/bake-floor-texture.py` renders one: orthographic projection onto the
mesh's own ground plane, barycentric UV rasterisation of all 100k triangles with
a depth buffer, largest fully-covered square, then a half-shift cross-fade to
make it wrap. Re-run it if the source scan changes and **keep
`FOREST_FLOOR_UV_SCALE` in step with the script's reported `TILE SPAN`.**

### Laying a scan down as geometry always shows its crop edge

The first attempt placed the scan as a mesh around the rose with a radial alpha
fade. The scan's crop boundary is part of the model, so there is no radius at
which it stops reading as a cut-out island. Texture on the existing ground is
both leaner (0 triangles vs 100,000; 517 KB vs 6.3 MB) and has no edge to hide.

### Comments in this repo have drifted from the code more than once

Three found this session, all of which said the opposite of what ran:
- ground map: comment described "a mottled earth-and-litter map"; code set `map = null`
- fog: comment said forest deepens it; `targetDensity` was `0.002` unconditionally
- grass: `createForestFloor()` returns an empty group and `createGrassField` is
  **never called** -- there is no grass in the scene at all

**Verify against the code, not the comment.**

### `isFoliageMat` is not a safe gate for anything but cutout depth materials

It matches material names like `Material.001`, which on neem is the **trunk**.
Harmless for a depth material, wrong for a displacement hook. Wind uses a
separate positive/negative name test and defaults to no-wind.

Also: `Object3D.name` comes from the glTF **node**, not the mesh. They happen to
match in all four tree models here (checked), but a static probe of mesh names
is not automatically what the runtime sees.

## 2. What changed

### Day/night continuity
Nothing may switch on a threshold test against sun altitude. `celestial.js` now
returns **`sunW`** (smoothstep of solar altitude over `-0.12 .. 0.08`); the key
lights, the painting light and the foliage shading all crossfade on it. Both
lights stay live through the handover so shadows fade rather than jump to the
opposite bearing.

`sunW` is deliberately **not** `1 - nightFactor` -- nightFactor drives colour and
reaches 1 later.

Foliage shading direction cannot be crossfaded, only its strength: sun and moon
sit on opposite bearings, so averaging directions collapses to zero length and
`normalize()` blows up. The direction switches, and `uShadeAmount` is
`|sunStrength - moonStrength|`, which falls continuously to zero exactly where
they are equal -- so the flip lands where there is no shading to flip. Measured
across a full day at 400k samples: worst single-step change **0.545 before,
1.7e-4 after**.

### Contrast
- `scene.environmentIntensity` 0.35 -> **0.13**. It exists for metal reflections
  but was pouring diffuse irradiance into every shadowed surface. The four
  metallic materials carry `envMapIntensity: 2.7` to compensate exactly.
- ambient 0.015 -> 0.007; daytime hemi fill cut ~43%; key light +19%; shadow
  radius 1.2 -> 0.9.
- Net: shadows read at **6.7% of lit brightness instead of 16.2%** (~2.4x).
- `uBacksideShade` 0.38 -> **0.15**, terminator widened to
  `smoothstep(-0.60, 0.55)` with a second smoothing pass.
- Leaf `emissive` 0.24/0.95 -> 0.07/0.5. Emissive is added *before* the shade
  multiply, so a strong term floors the dark side and cancels the contrast.

### Night
Forest only; gallery modes still need the moon to carry the scene.
moon 2.00 -> **0.80**, hemi 0.130 -> **0.068**, midnight floor luminance
24.8 -> **13.6**.

### Horizon
Forest fog `0.002 -> 0.0075` (a tree at the 150 m rim goes from 8.6% to **71.8%**
fogged). The ground rim now fades **alpha** (105 -> 149.5 m) on top of a colour
dissolve (70 -> 140 m), because `fogColor` is a single colour while the sky's
horizon is warm toward the sun and cool away from it -- a matched edge met an
unmatched sky on the anti-sun side. Fading alpha lets the real sky through, so it
matches from every bearing. Needed `transparent: true` on the ground in forest.

### Wind
Ambient breeze (`AMBIENT_WIND_STRENGTH = 0.34`) with a gate that lulls to nothing
for 10-22 s roughly every 1.5 min and eases back over ~4 s, plus three detuned
sines. Drag-driven motion still adds on top.

Attaches to foliage materials only, never bark -- trunks verified rooted across
all four models. Current rates (at the real time base): clusters and canopies
both 0.344 Hz wave / 1.192 Hz flutter, canopies additionally 0.458 Hz branch.

### Floor
Flat colour -> baked scan texture, tiled from world XZ and **detiled**: sampled
twice, second copy rotated ~37 deg and offset, cross-faded on a mask far coarser
than the tile. One tile = 2.16 m. `C_FLOOR_FOREST_*` went near-neutral because it
multiplies a photograph now.

### Forest entry
Eases to `FOREST_ENTRY_SUN_ANGLE` (noon, matching the Day button) over 2.2 s
along the **short arc** -- the angle is cyclic, so a plain tween from evening
would wind backwards through the whole night. Motion pauses first so the tween
owns `sunAngle` outright.

The pause is **revocable, tagged `_motionPausedByForest`**: only a pause the
grove set is undone on exit, and any manual tap transfers ownership to the
visitor permanently. Otherwise leaving the forest would strand the portfolio
frozen with no indication why.

### Fixed bugs
- `toggleDoor` set `controls.autoRotate = true` unconditionally in both its open
  and close paths. **In the forest every tree is a door**, so any tree click
  silently undid the pause button. Both now respect `motionPaused`.
- `startedAt` in `addLongPressHandler` is never cleared after a press, so a stray
  `pointerleave` much later can still be read as a long-press release.
  `setMotionPaused` now pins `daySpeed` where the intent is unambiguous.
- Discover button hidden in forest (it jumps to a random door or painting; the
  forest has neither). Follows the existing `instaBtn` pattern -- set both at
  creation and on view switch.
